# FarmSmart: 30-second showcase script

**What we're saying:** FarmSmart helps a farmer plan the whole farm one field at a time. Check each field, add it to the farm, then use **Whole farm** to choose the crop mix with the best profit for the risk the farmer accepts. It supports the farmer's judgment and does not replace it.
**Format:** 30.0 s · 4K · 24 fps · no voice-over · original music · burned-in caption bar (small uppercase kicker and one main line). The cursor is scripted over the real app with real data. Every cut falls on a whole frame (f = frame number).

## Title card (0.00–1.50 s)
**FarmSmart** (wordmark) · *Smarter choices. Better seasons.* · A decision tool for farms with many fields.

## As recorded (16 Nov 2026)

`farmsmart_30s_wholefarm_4k24.mp4` (4K, 24 fps, 30.0 s) follows this script with one change, found by testing the
real page: on the sample farm in normal years no single change raises profit **and** lowers risk (the farm is already
well balanced). So the whole-farm part turns on the page's own stress test, "Test a bad stretch: driest years, prices
20 % lower", and says so on screen. There, Find the best plan changes one field (Boone: continuous corn to corn, soybean,
winter wheat with a hairy vetch cover crop): farm score 6.8 to 8.1, typical year +$6,700, bad year +$18,200, chance of
a losing year 18 % to 10 %. Story County is really added to a farm that held Boone and Hamilton. Final captions:
`farmsmart_30s_wholefarm.en.srt`.
